# StageMon 2027.1.3 - shorter startup license reminder

- The full trial remains 30 days, with no wait during the trial.
- Afterwards, the startup reminder uses a 5-second countdown instead of 60, only once per process. All features remain available after the countdown.
- A valid license removes the reminder and wait; successful activation immediately lifts a running countdown. Saved activations retain their existing offline rights.
- Trial duration, license formats and signatures, pricing and activation service remain unchanged. Updating does not restart the trial.
- License screens and current FR/EN notices are updated. Previous release notes remain historical.
- Physical circuits, phone listening, audio processing and LIVE are unchanged and are not started automatically.

Windows and macOS share the same licensing policy. Mac packages remain ad hoc, without claiming Apple notarization or native Intel acceptance on an M2 machine. Software tests do not qualify hardware audio interfaces or physical phone listening.
